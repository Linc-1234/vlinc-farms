VLINC FARMS AGRICULTURAL GUIDE

Crop Management & Production

A practical overview of selected crops, their requirements, management practices, growth periods and major uses.

Crop Category Climate & Soil Planting Requirements Water Requirements Farm Management Common Diseases & Pests Growth Period Harvest Period Major Uses
🌾 Millet Cereal Food Crop Warm climate and relatively dry conditions. Performs well in well-drained sandy or loamy soils. Healthy seeds, prepared soil, appropriate spacing and effective weed management. Moderate. Millet is relatively drought tolerant. Regular weeding, pest monitoring, soil management and timely harvesting. Downy mildew, smut, birds, stem borers and armyworms. 3–5 months Harvest when grains are mature and sufficiently dry. Food, flour, porridge, animal feed and beverages.
🫘 Cowpea Legume Food Crop Warm climate with well-drained sandy or loamy soil. Quality seeds, good soil preparation, appropriate spacing and weed control. Moderate. Prolonged waterlogging should be avoided. Weeding, pest monitoring, soil fertility management and timely harvesting. Aphids, pod borers, thrips, fungal diseases and storage pests. 2–4 months Harvest fresh pods or mature dry grains, depending on the intended use. Food, flour, animal feed and soil improvement.
🌾 Rice Cereal Food Crop Warm and humid conditions with fertile, moisture-retentive soil. Quality seed, nursery or direct planting, suitable spacing and weed control. Moderate to high, depending on the production system. Water management, fertilizer application, weed control and pest monitoring. Rice blast, bacterial diseases, stem borers and rice bugs. 3–6 months Harvest when grains reach maturity and appropriate moisture levels. Food, flour, animal feed and processed products.
🍠 Yam Tuber Crop Food Crop Warm tropical climate with fertile, loose and well-drained soil. Healthy yam setts, prepared mounds or ridges, proper spacing and good planting material. Moderate, particularly during establishment and active growth. Weeding, staking where required, soil management and pest monitoring. Yam beetles, nematodes, anthracnose and tuber rots. 7–12 months Harvest when the plant reaches maturity and foliage begins to senesce. Food, flour, chips and processed products.
🌱 Cassava Root Crop Food Crop Warm tropical conditions. Performs best in well-drained soils. Healthy stem cuttings, good spacing, prepared soil and suitable planting conditions. Moderate. Cassava has good drought tolerance once established. Weed control, pest monitoring, soil fertility management and proper harvesting. Cassava mosaic disease, bacterial blight and mealybugs. 8–18 months Harvest according to variety, maturity and intended use. Garri, fufu, flour, starch, animal feed and industrial products.
🌽 Maize Cereal Food & Feed Crop Warm climate with fertile, well-drained loamy soil. Quality seed, proper spacing, fertilizer and effective weed control. Moderate to high, especially during flowering and grain development. Weed control, fertilizer application, pest monitoring and timely harvesting. Fall armyworm, stem borers, maize streak disease and ear rots. 3–5 months Harvest fresh or dry depending on production purpose. Food, animal feed, flour, starch and industrial products.
🥜 Cashew Tree Crop Cash Crop Warm tropical climate with well-drained sandy or loamy soil. Healthy seedlings or grafted plants, adequate spacing and proper land preparation. Moderate. Young trees require adequate moisture. Pruning, weed control, pest management, soil fertility management and harvesting. Anthracnose, powdery mildew, tea mosquito bug and other pests. Begins producing in approximately 3–5 years. Mature nuts and cashew apples are harvested during the appropriate season. Cashew nuts, juice, processed foods and industrial products.
🍫 Cocoa Tree Crop Cash Crop Warm and humid climate with fertile, well-drained soil. Healthy seedlings, suitable shade, proper spacing and good soil preparation. Relatively high moisture requirement, while maintaining good drainage. Pruning, shade management, weed control, sanitation and disease monitoring. Black pod disease, cocoa swollen shoot disease and insect pests. Usually begins producing after several years and can remain productive for many years. Mature cocoa pods are harvested at the appropriate stage. Chocolate, cocoa powder, cocoa butter and beverages.
☕ Coffee Coffea Cash / Beverage Crop Suitable temperature, adequate rainfall and fertile, well-drained soil. Healthy seedlings, proper spacing, suitable shade and good soil preparation. Moderate and consistent moisture, depending on variety and environment. Pruning, weed management, soil fertility management and pest control. Coffee leaf rust, berry disease, coffee berry borer and other pests. Often begins producing after approximately 3–4 years. Ripe coffee cherries are harvested according to the production system. Coffee beverages, roasted coffee and processed products.
🥬 Vegetables Horticulture Food Crop Varies by vegetable. Most perform well in fertile and well-drained soil. Quality seeds or seedlings, suitable spacing and good soil preparation. Regular moisture is generally important, especially during establishment. Watering, weeding, fertilization, pest monitoring and harvesting. Aphids, caterpillars, mites, fungal and bacterial diseases. Approximately 1–4 months, depending on the vegetable. Harvest at the appropriate maturity stage. Fresh food, processing, restaurants and household consumption.
Important Agricultural Note: Crop growth periods, water requirements, disease pressure, yields and market values can vary according to crop variety, soil conditions, climate, farming practices, season and location. This guide is intended as general agricultural information and should not replace professional agronomic advice.
