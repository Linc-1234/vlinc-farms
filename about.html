WHO WE ARE

About VLINC FARMS

Growing agriculture through quality production, innovation, sustainability and responsible farm management.

OUR STORY

Agriculture With Purpose

Vlinc Farms is a modern agricultural enterprise established in 2020 with a commitment to advancing agriculture through quality production, innovation, sustainability, and responsible farm management.

Since its establishment, Vlinc Farms has continued to grow by improving its farming methods, developing modern facilities, and adopting practices that enhance productivity and product quality.

The farm operates in both livestock production and crop farming, providing a diverse range of agricultural products.

WHAT WE DO

Our Agricultural Activities

Our operations combine livestock production, crop cultivation and modern agricultural practices.

🐄

Livestock Production

Our livestock activities include poultry, dairy animals, cattle, sheep, and other farm animals.

These animals are managed with proper attention to feeding, housing, hygiene, health, and welfare to ensure efficient and responsible production.

🌾

Crop Farming

Vlinc Farms cultivates a variety of food and cash crops including millet, cowpea, cashew, cocoa, yam, coffee (Coffea), rice, and other agricultural products.

Through crop diversity and appropriate farming practices, the farm contributes to food production, economic development, and agricultural sustainability.

🚜

Modern Agriculture

One of our major strengths is our investment in modern agricultural facilities and improved farming techniques.

These facilities support crop cultivation, livestock management, storage, processing, and other essential farm operations.
