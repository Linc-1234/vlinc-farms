🐄 Cattle Large Livestock Clean shelter, shade, ventilation, clean water, vaccination and parasite control. Feeding, watering, cleaning housing, health checks, weight monitoring and grooming. Foot-and-mouth disease, anthrax, mastitis, trypanosomiasis and respiratory infections. Calf Approximately 18–24 days. Usually occurs around 10–12 hours after standing heat. Grass, pasture, hay, silage, legumes, concentrates and minerals. Approximately 18–30 months to market maturity, depending on breed and feeding. ₦500,000 – ₦2,000,000+
🐄 Dairy Cattle Dairy Production Clean milking areas, good ventilation, comfortable housing and proper nutrition. Milking, feeding, watering, udder inspection, sanitation and health monitoring. Mastitis, milk fever, ketosis, parasites and reproductive disorders. Calf Approximately 18–24 days. Occurs after oestrus; accurate heat detection is important for breeding. Quality forage, hay, silage, grains, protein supplements and minerals. Productive lifespan can extend for several years with proper management. ₦600,000 – ₦2,500,000+
🐑 Sheep Small Ruminant Dry shelter, clean water, pasture, vaccination and parasite prevention. Feeding, watering, cleaning pens, hoof inspection and health checks. Foot rot, pneumonia, worms, enterotoxemia and other infections. Lamb Approximately 17 days. Generally occurs toward the end of oestrus. Pasture, grasses, hay, legumes, grains and mineral supplements. Approximately 6–12 months to market size for many meat breeds. ₦100,000 – ₦350,000+
🐐 Goats Small Ruminant Dry shelter, ventilation, clean water, parasite control and adequate space. Feeding, watering, hoof checks, cleaning pens and monitoring health. PPR, pneumonia, worms, coccidiosis and foot problems. Kid Approximately 18–22 days. Usually occurs near the end of oestrus. Browse plants, grasses, hay, legumes, concentrates and minerals. Approximately 6–12 months for many meat breeds. ₦80,000 – ₦300,000+
🐖 Pigs Monogastric Clean housing, good ventilation, temperature control and sufficient space. Feeding, watering, cleaning pens, growth monitoring and health inspection. African swine fever, pneumonia, diarrhoea, parasites and bacterial infections. Piglet Approximately 18–24 days. Ovulation occurs during oestrus; breeding should be properly timed. Balanced pig feed containing protein, energy, vitamins and minerals. Approximately 5–7 months to common market weight. ₦120,000 – ₦450,000+
🐔 Poultry Birds Clean housing, ventilation, appropriate temperature, lighting and biosecurity. Feeding, watering, cleaning litter, egg collection and bird inspection. Newcastle disease, coccidiosis, fowl pox and respiratory infections. Chick Poultry do not have a mammalian oestrus cycle. Laying hens ovulate periodically, depending on breed and conditions. Balanced poultry feed containing protein, energy, vitamins and minerals. Broilers can reach market size in approximately 6–8 weeks. ₦8,000 – ₦40,000+
🐇 Rabbits Small Livestock Clean cages, ventilation, protection from excessive heat and constant clean water. Feeding, watering, cage cleaning, health inspection and breeding checks. Coccidiosis, diarrhoea, ear mites, respiratory and digestive problems. Kit Rabbits are induced ovulators and do not have a fixed mammalian oestrus cycle. Ovulation is generally triggered by mating. Hay, grasses, leafy vegetables and balanced rabbit pellets. Approximately 10–14 weeks for many meat rabbits. ₦15,000 – ₦60,000+
🐟 Fish Aquaculture Good water quality, adequate oxygen, suitable stocking density and clean ponds. Feeding, water-quality monitoring, removing dead fish and checking growth. Bacterial infections, fungal infections, parasites and oxygen depletion. Fry / Fingerling Fish do not have a mammalian oestrus cycle. Reproduction varies according to species and environmental conditions. Species-specific commercial fish feed with appropriate protein levels. Many farmed species reach market size within approximately 6–12 months. ₦1,000 – ₦15,000+
Important Note: Prices are estimated Nigerian market ranges and may vary according to breed, age, weight, location, season and market conditions. Reproductive information may also vary by species and breed.
OUR APPROACH

Responsible Livestock Management

Healthy animals require consistent care and proper management throughout their growth and production cycles.

💧

Clean Water

Providing animals with access to clean and suitable drinking water.

🌾

Proper Feeding

Providing appropriate feeds and nutrients according to animal type and production stage.

🩺

Health Monitoring

Regular observation, hygiene, vaccination and disease prevention support healthier livestock.

🏡

Proper Housing

Clean, safe and suitable housing helps livestock remain comfortable and productive.
